WA Archiver turns your exported WhatsApp chats into a beautiful, private reading experience — no account, no cloud, no tracking.

IMPORT IN SECONDS
Export any chat from WhatsApp (chat → ⋮ → More → Export chat → with media) and drop the .zip or .txt into the app. Preview before import, multiple chats at once, media included.

A FAMILIAR, POLISHED CHAT UI
Bubbles, replies, reactions, photos, voice notes with waveforms, documents, locations, links and stickers — faithfully recreated, with light/dark themes, custom accents, wallpapers and bubble styles.

FIND ANYTHING
Global search (Ctrl/⌘+K), per-chat filters (date ranges, starred, regex), jump-to-date calendar, word clouds and an activity heatmap.

UNDERSTAND YOUR CHATS
Cross-chat Insights dashboard, per-chat statistics, year-in-review rewind, storage breakdown and "On this day" memories.

VOICE NOTES, TRANSCRIBED ON-DEVICE
Optional Whisper transcription runs fully offline — the model downloads once, then works in airplane mode.

OPTIONAL AI TOOLBOX (YOUR KEY)
Translate, explain slang, TL;DR a 10k-message group, ask AI about the chat or generate wallpapers — powered by your own Gemini API key, talking straight to Google.

PRIVATE BY ARCHITECTURE
Everything is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. No analytics, no trackers, no accounts. PIN app lock is verified locally. Export back out to txt/zip/standalone HTML/JSON at any time — your archive, your rules.

100% free and open source (MIT).
